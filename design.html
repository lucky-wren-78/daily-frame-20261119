1 · SHENZHEN, CHINA — APEC LEAD STORY · 90 MINUTES AT KYLIN VILLA

Three rivals share a table — and sign a declaration of peace

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met three-way for the first time ever, on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen — about 90 minutes at Kylin Villa, after separate Trump–Putin and Trump–Xi meetings.

Out of it came a four-point “Declaration Towards Peace”: a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought, with talks on lowering nuclear risk; more refined petroleum production and supply; reform of the UN Security Council; and a warning that the ICC must not abuse its jurisdiction.

What it did not do: set any timetable or figure — and it said nothing at all about Ukraine.

THE HAGUE · INTERNATIONAL LAW

Genocide, the court rules — a first for any state

The International Court of Justice ruled 9–6 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017 — the first time in the court’s 80-year history it has held a state responsible. About 10,000 Rohingya were killed and 392 villages burned; Myanmar must prosecute suspects and pay reparations.

NEW YORK · MARKETS

ANT’s first day: $111.50 open, $97.17 close, 17% up

The biggest IPO ever hit the Nasdaq: 980 million shares priced at $83, about $81.3 billion raised. The first trade printed 34% above the IPO price, the shares peaked at $115.10 and closed at $97.17; 846 million shares traded — the day’s busiest name. The Nasdaq fell 2.25%.

4
WORLDWIDE · GAMES

GTA VI is out — about 19 million copies, $1.84 billion

Grand Theft Auto VI went on sale at midnight in every region, the rollout beginning in New Zealand, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No official figures, but Sensor Tower put launch-day sales at 19.05 million copies and about $1.84 billion — more than twice GTA V’s first day in 2013.

5
RIYADH · CONFLICT

Missile strike scars the wealth fund’s tower

A Houthi missile struck the PIF Tower — the 80-storey, 385-metre headquarters of Saudi Arabia’s sovereign wealth fund — blowing out windows across seven to eight floors. Civil defence reported material damage and no injuries; the coalition said it dealt with a missile threat.

6
DHAKA · UNREST

Bangladesh’s lockdown day ends in fire and arrests

The banned Awami League’s nationwide lockdown had mixed effect — Dhaka mostly kept moving — but crude bombs went off in six districts, buses were torched and the ancestral home of former interim leader Muhammad Yunus was set alight. Two ruling-party members were killed; 97 Border Guard platoons deployed, 565 arrested in Dhaka.

7
CALIFORNIA · CHIPS

Nvidia subpoenaed over its ex-chief’s alleged affair

Attorney General Rob Bonta served an investigative subpoena on Nvidia over reports that former CEO Jensen Huang had an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy. Huang resigned last week after revelations of affairs, two with Nvidia employees; the board has ruled him out, and the SEC is said to be probing the firm.
