1 · ASIA · SUMMIT

APEC day two: leaders gather as a first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral looms

Xi Jinping addressed the APEC plenary in Shenzhen on Thursday morning, hours before a scheduled bilateral with Donald Trump at 12:45. The first-ever three-way meeting of Trump, Xi and Vladimir Putin is due at about 14:30 at the Kylin Villa, with Ukraine, the Iran war, trade, AI and nuclear weapons on the agenda. No summit decisions had been announced by the morning.

2 NORTH AMERICA · GAMES

GTA VI launches worldwide at midnight

Grand Theft Auto VI went on sale at midnight local time in every region, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with the rollout beginning in New Zealand. The standard edition costs $79.99; there is no PC date yet. It follows a day after reviews scored it 99 on Metacritic.

3 NORTH AMERICA · MARKETS

Anthropic debuts on the Nasdaq as ANT

Shares of the AI developer start trading on the Nasdaq today, a day after pricing at $83 in an enlarged 980-million-share offering worth about $81.3bn — the largest IPO ever, valuing the company at about $1.5tn. The co-founders ring the opening bell.

4 ASIA · UNREST

Bangladesh: lockdown day brings bombs and arrests

The Awami League’s nationwide lockdown fell on Thursday, with crude-bomb blasts in Dhaka and 565 people arrested in the capital in 24 hours, including 71 party and student-wing members. Overnight, interim leader Yunus’s ancestral home in Chattogram was torched.

5 ASIA · EDUCATION

551,864 Koreans sit the CSAT exam

South Korea’s university entrance exam ran 08:40–17:45 at 1,312 centres, with glasses and button inspections and 8,400 extra supervisors to stop AI cheating. Flights were halted 13:05–13:40 for the English listening test; the market opened an hour late.

6 ASIA · WEATHER

Akvan eases; landfall now seen at 90–110 km/h Friday

The India Meteorological Department scaled down its forecast in the 05:30 bulletin: the storm should cross between Narsapur and Puri by noon Friday as a severe cyclone, not a very severe one. Odisha shut schools in six districts for three days and evacuated 50,000 people.

7 ASIA · DIPLOMACY

India and Japan hold their fourth 2+2 in Tokyo

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and External Affairs Minister Jaishankar met Japan’s Koizumi and Motegi to review defence and strategic cooperation and a free and open Indo-Pacific. It is Singh’s third bilateral with Koizumi this year.
